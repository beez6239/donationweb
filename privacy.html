Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Practices

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AmericaYouandI collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit or interact with our website and services. The policy applies to visitors, donors, volunteers, and partners.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information you provide directly (such as name, email, postal address, and payment details) and information collected automatically (such as IP address, device and browser data, and cookies).

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to process donations, communicate with you, improve our services, personalize content, and for security and fraud prevention. We may also use data for analytics and reporting.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, analyze site usage, and deliver relevant content. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers, payment processors, and partners who assist in delivering our services. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please exercise caution when sharing sensitive information online.

7. Your Rights

You may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data depending on your jurisdiction. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Our site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email info@americayouandi.com or use our contact page.
