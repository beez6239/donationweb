About Us

AmericaYouandI is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating hope, equality, and opportunity for those in need. We believe that real change begins when communities come together united by compassion and action. Through our programs in education, healthcare, and humanitarian aid, we strive to make a lasting difference in the lives of children, families, and communities around the world.
